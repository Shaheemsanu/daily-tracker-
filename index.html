How reports work

Done = completed tasks. Not done = tasks explicitly marked not done. Pending = tasks left unfinished without a status decision. Completion rate is completed tasks divided by all tasks with a final status (done or not done). Habit completion counts completed check trackers and counters that meet their daily goal.
